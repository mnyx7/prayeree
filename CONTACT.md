# Contact and support

**Application:** Islamic Companion (`prayeree`)

**Developer:** Minaya

**GitHub:** [mnyx7](https://github.com/mnyx7)

**Repository:** [mnyx7/prayeree](https://github.com/mnyx7/prayeree)

## Technical support

If repository Issues are enabled and accessible, [open an issue](https://github.com/mnyx7/prayeree/issues/new) for bugs, feature requests, or documentation corrections.

Include the app version, iOS version, device model, steps to reproduce, expected behavior, and what happened. A screenshot can help. Avoid including tokens, passwords, precise location, or other personal information in a public issue.

For notifications, include whether permission was enabled and whether the issue occurred with the app open, in the background, or closed. For prayer times, the city and selected calculation method/madhab are generally sufficient; exact GPS coordinates are not necessary for an initial report.

## Privacy requests

See the [privacy policy draft](PRIVACY_POLICY.md). This build stores reading progress, favorites, and preferences on the device and does not provide developer-operated accounts. Removing the app removes its local app container, subject to Apple's backup/restore behavior.

A private support/privacy email has not yet been provided. Before App Store release, the developer must publish and verify a reachable contact route, including a private channel suitable for sensitive requests. If this repository is private, GitHub Issues cannot be used as the app's public support route.

## Content corrections and rights

For a translation/content concern, identify the surah and ayah reference, the selected translation or edition, and the nature of the concern. Do not post an entire copyrighted translation as an attachment. Provider copyright and licensing requests should also be directed to the relevant rights holder or Quran Foundation through its [official documentation/contact information](https://api-docs.quran.com/legal/developer-terms/).

This file is repository documentation, not a promise of a support response time or a finalized public support service.
