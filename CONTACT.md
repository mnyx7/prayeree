## Technical support

If repository Issues are enabled and accessible, [open an issue](https://github.com/mnyx7/prayeree/issues/new) for bugs, feature requests, or documentation corrections.

Include the app version, iOS version, device model, steps to reproduce, expected behavior, and what happened. A screenshot can help. Avoid including tokens, passwords, precise location, or other personal information in a public issue.

For notifications, say whether the alert was for a prayer, sunrise, or the daily Quran reminder; include whether permission was enabled and whether the issue occurred with the app open, in the background, or closed. For prayer or sunrise times, include the city, calendar date, timezone, selected source/calculation method, and any prayer adjustments. Madhab is relevant for calculated prayer times; Minber uses a fixed city timetable. Exact GPS coordinates are not necessary for an initial report.

## Privacy requests

See the [privacy policy](PRIVACY_POLICY.md). This build stores reading progress, favorites, and preferences on the device and does not provide developer-operated accounts. Removing the app removes its local app container, subject to Apple's backup/restore behavior.

For private support and privacy requests, email minaya.yagubova@icloud.com. GitHub documentation links must be accessible without signing in before using them as public App Store URLs.

## Content corrections and rights

For a translation/content concern, identify the surah and ayah reference, the selected translation or edition, and the nature of the concern. Do not post an entire copyrighted translation as an attachment. Provider copyright and licensing requests should also be directed to the relevant rights holder or Quran Foundation through its [official documentation/contact information](https://api-docs.quran.com/legal/developer-terms/).

For a Minber/QMİ timetable discrepancy, include the city, date, and displayed time, and compare it with [Minber’s published timetable](https://namaz.minber.az/). Minber is a third-party distributor of QMİ-based timetables. API publishing/redistribution permission and provider corrections should be confirmed with the operator; the app does not claim QMİ endorsement.

No support response time is guaranteed.
